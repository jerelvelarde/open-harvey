# Order Form and Subscription Terms

Brightwater Metrics, Inc. and Northstar Software, Inc. · Order BWM-0418 · received November 10, 2026

## 1. Parties and order

**1.1 Parties.** This Order Form is between Brightwater Metrics, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Brightwater"), and Northstar Software, Inc., a California corporation ("Northstar").

**1.2 Order of precedence.** These subscription terms and this Order Form are the whole agreement. Brightwater's website terms do not apply.

## 2. Subscription and term

**2.1 Service.** Brightwater Product Analytics for up to 25 million events a month, used by Northstar's product teams.

**2.2 Term.** The subscription runs for twenty-four (24) months from the start date.

**2.3 Renewal.** The subscription renews for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives notice at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Fees

**3.1 Fees.** $84,000 per year, invoiced annually in advance. Prices are fixed for the first term.

**3.2 Payment.** Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

## 4. Data and security

**4.1 Customer Data.** Northstar will send event data about how its customers use Northstar's products, which includes personal data such as user ids and email addresses.

**4.2 Data processing.** Brightwater will process Customer Data only under the Data Processing Addendum attached as Exhibit A, signed with this Order Form and in effect before any processing.

**4.3 Security.** Brightwater maintains a security program with encryption in transit and at rest, single sign-on for staff, and annual staff training.

**4.4 Incidents.** Brightwater will notify Northstar of any unauthorized access to Customer Data within forty-eight (48) hours of discovering it.

**4.5 Usage data.** Northstar owns Customer Data and all reports and outputs. Brightwater may use only aggregated, de-identified metrics to operate and improve the service.

## 5. Liability and indemnity

**5.1 Cap.** Each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months before the claim.

**5.2 IP indemnity.** Brightwater will defend Northstar against third-party claims that the service infringes intellectual property rights. This obligation is not subject to the cap.

## 6. Confidentiality

**6.1 Confidentiality.** Each party protects the other's confidential information with reasonable care, during the term and for three (3) years after it ends.

## 7. Termination and assignment

**7.1 Termination for convenience.** Northstar may end the subscription for convenience on sixty (60) days' written notice, with a refund of prepaid fees for the unused period.

**7.2 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Order Form without consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of its business.

## 8. Insurance and law

**8.1 Insurance.** Brightwater carries cyber liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 per claim and general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000.

**8.2 Governing law.** Delaware law governs, and disputes go to the state or federal courts in Wilmington, Delaware.
