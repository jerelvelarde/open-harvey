# Master Services Agreement

Acme Cloud, Inc. and Northstar Software, Inc. · Acme form MSA-2026-3 · received November 5, 2026

## 1. Parties and order of precedence

**1.1 Parties.** This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is between Acme Cloud, Inc., a Delaware corporation with offices in Portland, Oregon ("Acme"), and Northstar Software, Inc., a California corporation ("Northstar"). Acme and Northstar are each a "party" and together the "parties".

**1.2 Order Forms.** Acme provides the Services described in one or more order forms signed by both parties ("Order Forms"). Each Order Form is part of this Agreement. The first Order Form covers Acme Observability for Northstar's Platform Infrastructure team, for up to 40 terabytes of ingested data a month.

**1.3 Order of precedence.** If this Agreement and an Order Form conflict, this Agreement controls, unless the Order Form names the section of this Agreement it changes. Acme's online terms, policies and click-through notices do not apply to Northstar, even if a Northstar user accepts them.

## 2. The Services

**2.1 Acme Observability.** Acme will provide its hosted observability platform, Acme Observability, which collects, stores and searches the logs, metrics and traces that Northstar's systems send to it (the "Services"). Northstar may use the Services for its internal business purposes, including running and supporting its own products.

**2.2 Support.** Acme will provide support by email and chat at all hours for issues that stop the Services from working, and during Acme's business hours for everything else. Acme will name a support manager for Northstar's account.

**2.3 Service levels.** Acme will make the Services available at least 99.9% of each calendar month, not counting scheduled maintenance announced at least five days in advance. If Acme misses that target, Northstar receives the service credits set out in the Order Form, which are Northstar's only remedy for the missed target.

**2.4 Changes to the Services.** Acme may update the Services from time to time, but will not materially reduce their core functions during a term.

**2.5 Acceptable use.** Northstar will not use the Services to store payment card numbers, health records or government identification numbers, and will not try to copy, resell or reverse engineer the Services.

## 3. Term and renewal

**3.1 Effective date.** This Agreement starts on the date the second party signs it (the "Effective Date") and continues until every Order Form under it has ended.

**3.2 Initial term.** The initial term of the first Order Form is thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date.

**3.3 Renewal.** Each Order Form renews automatically for successive thirty-six (36) month terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 4. Fees and payment

**4.1 Fees.** Northstar will pay Acme $240,000 per year for the Services in the first Order Form, invoiced annually in advance. Ingesting more than the volume in the Order Form is billed monthly in arrears at the overage rate in that Order Form.

**4.2 Payment terms.** Northstar will pay each undisputed invoice within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date. Amounts not paid when due accrue interest at 1% per month, and Acme may suspend the Services if an undisputed amount is more than thirty days late.

**4.3 Price changes.** Acme may increase the fees for any renewal term by up to 9% a year by giving written notice at least sixty (60) days before the renewal.

**4.4 Taxes.** Fees do not include taxes. Each party pays the taxes the law places on it, and Northstar is not responsible for taxes on Acme's income.

**4.5 Disputed invoices.** Northstar may dispute an invoice in good faith by telling Acme before it is due. The parties will work to resolve the dispute within thirty days, and Acme will not suspend the Services over a disputed amount.

## 5. Security

**5.1 Security program.** Acme will maintain a written information security program with administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the Services, including encryption of Customer Data in transit and at rest, access controls, and logging of administrative access.

**5.2 Security reviews.** Once a year, Acme will answer Northstar's reasonable security questionnaire and describe any material change to its safeguards since the previous answer.

**5.3 Personnel.** Acme will run background checks, where the law allows, on staff who can access Customer Data, and will train them on security at least once a year.

## 6. Data protection

**6.1 Processing of Customer Data.** "Customer Data" means the data that Northstar or its users send to the Services, which may include personal data about Northstar's customers, such as names, email addresses and IP addresses that appear in logs. Acme will process Customer Data to provide and support the Services and as otherwise permitted by this Agreement.

**6.2 Data location.** Acme will store Customer Data in data centers located in the United States and will not move it outside the United States without Northstar's written consent.

**6.3 Subprocessors.** Acme may use subprocessors to provide the Services and will list them on request. Acme remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subprocessors.

**6.4 Security incidents.** If Acme confirms that an unauthorized person has accessed Customer Data (a "Security Incident"), Acme will notify Northstar within ten (10) days and will share what it knows about the cause, the data affected and the steps it is taking.

**6.5 Assistance.** Acme will reasonably help Northstar respond to requests from individuals about their personal data and to questions from regulators about the Services.

## 7. Intellectual property and usage data

**7.1 Acme property.** Acme owns the Services, its software and documentation, and all improvements to them, including improvements suggested by Northstar.

**7.2 Customer Data.** As between the parties, Northstar owns Customer Data. Northstar grants Acme the rights to Customer Data needed to provide the Services.

**7.3 Usage data.** Acme owns all usage data and derived insights generated from the operation of the Services, including information derived from Customer Data, and may use them for any purpose during and after the term.

## 8. Limitation of liability

**8.1 No indirect damages.** Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages, or for lost profits or revenue, even if it was told they were possible.

**8.2 Cap.** Each party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid by Northstar in the three (3) months before the event giving rise to the claim.

**8.3 Exceptions.** The limits in this Section 8 do not apply to a party's indemnity obligations under Section 9 or to its gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct. Claims for breach of Section 10 and claims arising from a Security Incident remain subject to the cap in Section 8.2.

## 9. Indemnities

**9.1 By Acme.** Acme will defend Northstar against any third-party claim that the Services infringe or misappropriate that third party's intellectual property rights, and will pay the damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees finally awarded or agreed in settlement.

**9.2 By Northstar.** Northstar will defend Acme against any third-party claim arising from Customer Data that Northstar did not have the right to send to the Services, and will pay the damages and costs finally awarded or agreed in settlement.

**9.3 Procedure.** The party seeking defense must notify the other party promptly, let it control the defense and settlement, and cooperate reasonably at its expense.

**9.4 Remedies.** If the Services are, or Acme reasonably believes they may become, the subject of an infringement claim, Acme may modify them, obtain the right for Northstar to keep using them, or, if neither is reasonable, end the affected Order Form and refund any prepaid fees for the rest of its term.

## 10. Confidentiality

**10.1 Obligations.** Each party will use the other's confidential information only to perform this Agreement, will protect it with at least reasonable care, and will share it only with employees and contractors who need to know it and are bound to keep it confidential.

**10.2 Duration.** These obligations apply during the term and for five (5) years after it ends, and to trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets.

**10.3 Exclusions.** Confidential information does not include information that becomes public through no fault of the receiving party, was already known to it, is developed independently, or is received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality.

**10.4 Required disclosure.** A party may disclose confidential information when the law requires it, after giving the other party notice where the law allows.

## 11. Termination

**11.1 For cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days of written notice describing it.

**11.2 No termination for convenience.** Neither party may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form for convenience before the end of the then-current term, and fees for the full term remain payable.

**11.3 Effect of termination.** When this Agreement ends, Acme will make Customer Data available for export for thirty (30) days and will then delete it from its systems.

**11.4 Survival.** Sections 4, 7, 8, 9, 10 and 14 survive the end of this Agreement.

## 12. Assignment

**12.1 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's written consent, except to a successor in a merger, acquisition or sale of substantially all of its assets, with written notice to the other party.

**12.2 Subcontracting.** Acme may use subcontractors to perform this Agreement, but remains responsible for their work as if it were its own.

## 13. Insurance

**13.1 Coverage.** During the term, Acme will carry commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and cyber liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 per claim, with insurers rated A- or better, and will provide certificates on request.

**13.2 Changes in coverage.** Acme will tell Northstar within thirty (30) days if any required policy is cancelled or reduced below these amounts.

## 14. Governing law and general terms

**14.1 Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The state and federal courts in San Francisco County have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute.

**14.2 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and sent to the addresses in the Order Form, with a copy by email to each party's legal contact.

**14.3 Entire agreement.** This Agreement and its Order Forms are the parties' entire agreement on their subject and replace any earlier proposals or discussions. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.


**14.4 Force majeure.** Neither party is liable for a delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, such as natural disasters or widespread internet outages, except for its obligations to pay.

**14.5 Relationship.** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency, and neither party may bind the other.
