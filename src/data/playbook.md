# Northstar Software: Vendor Contract Playbook (2026)

Fictional playbook for the workshop. Northstar's positions for vendor contracts, one rule per line, each with an id the copilot cites. Legal reviews every vendor contract against these rules; anything that does not meet a rule is a deviation.

## Commercial terms

**PB-REN-01 Renewal.** A contract may renew automatically for no more than 12 months at a time, and Northstar must be able to cancel with no more than 30 days' notice before the renewal.

**PB-PAY-01 Payment.** Invoices are payable net 45 or longer from the invoice date.

**PB-LIAB-01 Limitation of liability.** The vendor's liability cap is at least the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim.

**PB-IND-01 Indemnities.** The vendor defends Northstar against third-party intellectual property claims, and that obligation sits outside the liability cap.

## Data and security

**PB-DATA-01 Data protection.** If the vendor will process personal data about Northstar's customers, a signed data processing addendum (DPA) is in place before any processing.

**PB-DATA-02 Security incidents.** The vendor notifies Northstar of a security incident affecting Northstar data within 72 hours.

**PB-IP-01 Data and outputs.** Northstar owns its data and all outputs. The vendor may use only aggregated, de-identified metrics, and only to run and improve its service.

## Relationship

**PB-CONF-01 Confidentiality.** Confidentiality is mutual and lasts at least 3 years after the contract ends.

**PB-TERM-01 Termination.** Northstar may terminate for convenience on no more than 30 days' notice.

**PB-ASSIGN-01 Assignment.** Neither party may assign the contract without consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of its business.

**PB-INS-01 Insurance.** The vendor carries cyber liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 and general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000.

**PB-LAW-01 Governing law.** California or Delaware law governs, with disputes in that state's courts.

## Approvals

Approvers approve Northstar's position before anything goes to the vendor. Nothing is signed until Northstar and the vendor sign the agreement.

**PB-APR-01 General Counsel.** When a vendor's contract deviates from this playbook, the General Counsel approves Northstar's position before it goes to the vendor, including the reason for any position Northstar accepts below the playbook.

**PB-APR-02 Head of Security.** When a deviation touches data protection, security or customer data (PB-DATA-01, PB-DATA-02, PB-IP-01 for usage data, and any security rule), the Head of Security also approves it.

**PB-APR-03 CFO.** When the total contract value is above $500,000, the CFO also approves the spend. Total contract value is the annual fee times the initial term.

Nothing goes to the vendor until every approver on the route has approved it.
