# Data Enrichment Services Agreement

Fernway Data, Inc. and Northstar Software, Inc. · Northstar matter MAT-2026-040 · signed October 2, 2026

## 1. Parties and services

**1.1 Parties.** This Data Enrichment Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is between Fernway Data, Inc., a Texas corporation ("Fernway"), and Northstar Software, Inc., a California corporation ("Northstar").

**1.2 Services.** Fernway will match Northstar's customer account records against its licensed business directories and return company details, such as size, industry and region, through a private API (the "Services").
## 2. Term and renewal

**2.1 Initial term.** The initial term is thirty-six (36) months from October 2, 2026.

**2.2 Renewal.** This Agreement then renews for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the current term ends.

**2.3 Source commitments.** Fernway licenses its directory sources a year at a time, and the longer notice periods in Sections 2.2 and 7.1 give it time to adjust those commitments.

## 3. Fees and payment

**3.1 Fees.** Northstar will pay Fernway $180,000 per year, invoiced annually in advance. Fees will not increase during the initial term.

**3.2 Payment.** Invoices are due within forty-five (45) days of the invoice date.

## 4. Data protection

**4.1 Customer Data.** The records Northstar sends Fernway will include personal data about Northstar's customers, such as contact names, work email addresses and job titles ("Customer Data").

**4.2 Signed DPA.** The parties have signed a data processing addendum, and Fernway will process Customer Data only as that addendum and Northstar's written instructions allow.

**4.3 No resale.** Fernway will not sell Customer Data, add it to its directories, or use it to enrich records for other clients. It may use only aggregated, de-identified statistics to run and improve the Services.

**4.4 Incident notice.** Fernway will notify Northstar within forty-eight (48) hours of learning of any security incident affecting Customer Data.

## 5. Security

**5.1 Security program.** Fernway will run a documented security program covering encryption of Customer Data in transit and at rest, least-privilege access with multi-factor sign-in, a log of every access to Customer Data, and a named security lead.

**5.2 Retention.** Fernway will delete each batch of Customer Data within thirty (30) days after it returns the enriched results.

## 6. Liability and indemnity

**6.1 Cap.** Each party's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months before the claim.

**6.2 IP indemnity.** Fernway will defend Northstar against third-party claims that the Services or Fernway's directory data infringe intellectual property rights, and this duty sits outside the cap in Section 6.1.
## 7. Termination

**7.1 For convenience.** Northstar may terminate this Agreement for convenience on sixty (60) days' written notice, with a refund of prepaid fees for any full months that remain.

**7.2 For cause.** Either party may terminate if the other fails to cure a material breach within thirty (30) days of written notice.

## 8. General terms

**8.1 Confidentiality.** Each party will protect the other's confidential information during the term and for three (3) years after it ends.

**8.2 Insurance.** Fernway will carry cyber liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 and general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000.

**8.3 Governing law.** Delaware law governs this Agreement, and disputes go to the state and federal courts in Delaware.

**8.4 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of its business.
