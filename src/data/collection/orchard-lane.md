# Recruiting Services Agreement

Orchard Lane Recruiting LLC and Northstar Software, Inc. · Northstar matter MAT-2026-023 · signed May 21, 2026

## 1. Parties and services

**1.1 Parties.** This Recruiting Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is between Orchard Lane Recruiting LLC, an Oregon limited liability company ("Orchard Lane"), and Northstar Software, Inc., a California corporation ("Northstar").

**1.2 Services.** Orchard Lane will find, screen and introduce candidates for the engineering and design roles that Northstar lists with it, and will schedule first-round interviews.

**1.3 Hiring decisions.** Northstar alone decides whom to interview and hire. Orchard Lane will not present a candidate to Northstar without that candidate's permission.

## 2. Term and renewal

**2.1 Term.** This Agreement runs for twelve (12) months from May 21, 2026.

**2.2 Renewal.** It then renews for successive six (6) month terms unless either party gives notice at least fifteen (15) days before the current term ends.

## 3. Fees

**3.1 Retainer.** Northstar will pay a retainer of $45,000 per year, billed quarterly, which covers up to twelve placements.

**3.2 Payment.** Northstar will pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days of receiving it.

**3.3 Replacement.** If a placed candidate leaves within ninety days of starting, Orchard Lane will search for a replacement at no added charge.

## 4. Candidate information

**4.1 Candidate records.** Orchard Lane collects resumes and contact details from candidates directly, and shares them with Northstar only for open roles.

**4.2 No customer data.** Orchard Lane handles no customer personal data, and Northstar will not give it access to any system that holds information about Northstar's customers.

**4.3 Incident notice.** If Northstar information or candidate records held by Orchard Lane are exposed to anyone without permission, Orchard Lane will tell Northstar within three (3) days.

## 5. Security

**5.1 Safeguards.** Orchard Lane will keep candidate records in an access-controlled applicant tracking system, require multi-factor sign-in for its recruiters, and remove a recruiter's access within one business day of their departure.

**5.2 Devices.** Recruiters working on Northstar roles will use company-managed laptops with full-disk encryption and automatic screen locks.

## 6. Liability and indemnity

**6.1 Cap.** Each party's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months before the claim.

**6.2 IP indemnity.** Orchard Lane will defend Northstar against third-party claims that any materials it provides infringe intellectual property rights, and this duty sits outside the cap.

**6.3 Non-solicitation.** Orchard Lane will not recruit Northstar employees for other clients during the term and for one year after it ends.

## 7. Termination

**7.1 For convenience.** Northstar may end this Agreement at any time on fifteen (15) days' written notice, paying only the retainer earned through the end date.

**7.2 For cause.** Either party may end this Agreement if the other materially breaches it and fails to cure within thirty (30) days of notice.

## 8. General terms

**8.1 Confidentiality.** Each party will protect the other's confidential information during the term and for three (3) years afterward.

**8.2 Insurance.** Orchard Lane will carry general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 and cyber liability insurance of at least $5,000,000.

**8.3 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of its business.

**8.4 Governing law.** Delaware law governs this Agreement, and disputes go to the state courts in Delaware.
