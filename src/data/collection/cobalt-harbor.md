# Log Management Subscription

Cobalt Harbor Logging, Inc. and Northstar Software, Inc. · Northstar matter MAT-2026-031 · signed July 24, 2026

## 1. Parties and subscription

**1.1 Parties.** This Log Management Subscription (the "Agreement") is between Cobalt Harbor Logging, Inc., a Washington corporation ("Cobalt Harbor"), and Northstar Software, Inc., a California corporation ("Northstar").

**1.2 Service.** Cobalt Harbor will collect, index and retain application logs from Northstar's production systems, and provide search, alerting and dashboards over them (the "Service").
## 2. Term and renewal

**2.1 Initial term.** The initial term is thirty-six (36) months from July 24, 2026.

**2.2 Renewal.** At the end of the initial term the subscription renews for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the period ends.

## 3. Fees and payment

**3.1 Fees.** Northstar will pay Cobalt Harbor $104,000 per year, invoiced annually in advance.

**3.2 Payment.** Invoices are payable within forty-five (45) days of receipt.
## 4. Customer data

**4.1 Personal data in logs.** Northstar's logs will contain personal data about Northstar's customers, including user IDs, email addresses and IP addresses ("Customer Data").

**4.2 Signed addendum.** Cobalt Harbor processes Customer Data only under the signed data processing addendum attached to this Agreement as Exhibit A.

**4.3 Ownership.** Northstar owns Customer Data and everything the Service produces from it. Cobalt Harbor may use only aggregated, de-identified service statistics, and only to run and improve the Service.

**4.4 Hosting.** Cobalt Harbor will store Customer Data only in data centers in the United States.

## 5. Security

**5.1 Security program.** Cobalt Harbor will maintain an information security program designed to protect Customer Data, with encryption in transit and at rest, single sign-on and multi-factor access for staff, and around-the-clock monitoring of its production environment.

**5.2 Incident notice.** Cobalt Harbor will notify Northstar of any security incident affecting Customer Data within twenty-four (24) hours of discovering it, and will give regular updates until it is resolved.

**5.3 Personnel.** Staff with access to Customer Data receive security training when hired and every year after.

## 6. Liability and indemnity

**6.1 Cap.** Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months before the claim.

**6.2 IP indemnity.** Cobalt Harbor will defend Northstar against third-party claims that the Service infringes intellectual property rights. This obligation is not subject to the cap.

**6.3 Indirect damages.** Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages or for lost profits.

## 7. Termination

**7.1 For convenience.** Northstar may terminate this Agreement for convenience with thirty (30) days' written notice, and Cobalt Harbor will refund prepaid fees for the remaining months.

**7.2 For cause.** Either party may terminate if the other does not cure a material breach within thirty (30) days of written notice.

**7.3 Deletion.** Within thirty (30) days after termination, Cobalt Harbor will delete Customer Data and confirm the deletion in writing.

## 8. General terms

**8.1 Confidentiality.** Confidentiality obligations are mutual and last for three (3) years after this Agreement ends.

**8.2 Insurance.** Cobalt Harbor will carry cyber liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 and general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000.

**8.3 Governing law.** Delaware law governs this Agreement, and disputes go to the state and federal courts in Delaware.

**8.4 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of its business.
