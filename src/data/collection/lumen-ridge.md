# Analytics Platform Subscription

Lumen Ridge Analytics, Inc. and Northstar Software, Inc. · Northstar matter MAT-2026-016 · signed April 20, 2026

## 1. Parties and platform

**1.1 Parties.** This Analytics Platform Subscription (the "Agreement") is between Lumen Ridge Analytics, Inc., a Colorado corporation ("Lumen Ridge"), and Northstar Software, Inc., a California corporation ("Northstar").

**1.2 Platform.** Lumen Ridge will provide its hosted analytics platform, which takes in product usage events from Northstar's applications and turns them into dashboards, funnels and retention reports (the "Platform").
## 2. Term and renewal

**2.1 Start date.** This Agreement begins on April 20, 2026.

**2.2 Initial term.** The initial term is thirty-six (36) months from the start date.

**2.3 Renewal.** The subscription then renews for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the current term ends.

## 3. Fees and payment

**3.1 Fees.** Northstar will pay Lumen Ridge $96,000 per year, invoiced annually in advance.

**3.2 Payment.** Invoices are due forty-five (45) days after the invoice date. Lumen Ridge will not raise fees during the initial term.

## 4. Data protection and security

**4.1 Customer Data.** "Customer Data" means the events and records Northstar sends to the Platform, which will include personal data about Northstar's customers, such as account IDs, email addresses and device details.

**4.2 Data processing addendum.** The data processing addendum signed by both parties on April 20, 2026 applies to all Customer Data and controls if it conflicts with this Agreement.

**4.3 Security program.** Lumen Ridge will maintain a written security program that includes encryption of Customer Data in transit and at rest, role-based access with multi-factor sign-in, logging of administrative actions, and yearly security training for its staff.

**4.4 Security incidents.** Lumen Ridge will notify Northstar within seventy-two (72) hours after it becomes aware of unauthorized access to Customer Data, and will share the cause and the records affected.

**4.5 Ownership.** Northstar owns Customer Data and every report built from it. Lumen Ridge may use only aggregated, de-identified figures, and only to operate and improve the Platform.

## 5. Limitation of liability

**5.1 Cap.** Each party's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months before the claim.
## 6. Indemnity and confidentiality

**6.1 IP indemnity.** Lumen Ridge will defend Northstar against any third-party claim that the Platform infringes intellectual property rights, and will pay the resulting damages and settlements.

**6.2 Confidentiality.** Each party will keep the other's confidential information private during the term and for three (3) years afterward.

## 7. Termination

**7.1 For convenience.** Northstar may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice and will receive a refund of prepaid fees for the unused period.

**7.2 For cause.** Either party may terminate on written notice if the other fails to cure a material breach within thirty (30) days.
## 8. General terms

**8.1 Insurance.** Lumen Ridge will carry general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 and cyber liability insurance of at least $3,000,000.

**8.2 Governing law.** New York law governs this Agreement, and disputes go to the state and federal courts in New York County.

**8.3 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of its business.
