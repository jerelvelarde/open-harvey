# Payroll Services Agreement

Halden Payroll Services, Inc. and Northstar Software, Inc. · Northstar matter MAT-2026-008 · signed February 12, 2026

## 1. Parties and services

**1.1 Parties.** This Payroll Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is between Halden Payroll Services, Inc., a Delaware corporation based in Columbus, Ohio ("Halden"), and Northstar Software, Inc., a California corporation ("Northstar").

**1.2 Services.** Halden will run payroll for Northstar's United States employees twice a month, file payroll tax returns in Northstar's name, and issue year-end wage statements (the "Services").

**1.3 Northstar's part.** Northstar will send Halden accurate hours, salary changes and new-hire details at least three business days before each pay date.

## 2. Term and renewal

**2.1 Initial term.** The initial term is twenty-four (24) months from February 12, 2026.

**2.2 Renewal.** After the initial term, this Agreement renews for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Fees and payment

**3.1 Fees.** Northstar will pay Halden $64,000 per year, invoiced monthly in equal parts. Year-end filings are included in that amount.

**3.2 Payment terms.** Northstar will pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

## 4. Data handling

**4.1 Employee records.** Halden will receive the names, addresses, tax identifiers and bank details of Northstar employees, and will use them only to provide the Services.

**4.2 No customer data.** Halden handles no customer personal data: it receives nothing about Northstar's customers, and Northstar will not send it any.

**4.3 Security incidents.** If Halden learns that someone without permission has reached Northstar employee records, it will tell Northstar within forty-eight (48) hours and keep Northstar informed until the matter is closed.

## 5. Security

**5.1 Safeguards.** Halden will keep employee records encrypted in transit and at rest, limit access to the staff who run Northstar's payroll, and require multi-factor sign-in for all of its systems.

**5.2 Questionnaire.** Once a year, Halden will complete Northstar's security questionnaire and tell Northstar of any material change to its safeguards.

## 6. Liability and indemnity

**6.1 Cap.** Each party's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months before the claim.

**6.2 IP indemnity.** Halden will defend Northstar against any third-party claim that the Services infringe intellectual property rights, and this duty is not limited by the cap.

## 7. Termination

**7.1 For convenience.** Northstar may terminate this Agreement for any reason on thirty (30) days' written notice, and will pay only for Services performed through the termination date.

**7.2 For cause.** Either party may terminate if the other materially breaches this Agreement and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days of notice.

**7.3 Transition.** On termination, Halden will return all employee records and then delete its copies within sixty (60) days.

## 8. General terms

**8.1 Confidentiality.** Each party will protect the other's confidential information and use it only for this Agreement, during the term and for three (3) years after.

**8.2 Insurance.** Halden will carry general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 and cyber liability insurance of at least $5,000,000.

**8.3 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of its business.

**8.4 Governing law.** California law governs this Agreement, and disputes go to the state and federal courts in San Francisco County.
