# Translation Services Agreement

Kestrel Language Services, Inc. and Northstar Software, Inc. · Northstar matter MAT-2026-035 · signed August 13, 2026

## 1. Parties and services

**1.1 Parties.** This Translation Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is between Kestrel Language Services, Inc., a Massachusetts corporation ("Kestrel"), and Northstar Software, Inc., a California corporation ("Northstar").

**1.2 Services.** Kestrel will translate Northstar's product interface text, help center articles and release notes into the languages Northstar requests, with a second linguist reviewing each job before delivery.

**1.3 Turnaround.** Kestrel will deliver jobs of up to 5,000 words within three business days of each request, and larger jobs on a schedule the parties agree.

## 2. Term and renewal

**2.1 Term.** This Agreement runs for twelve (12) months from August 13, 2026.

**2.2 Renewal.** It renews for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives written notice at least thirty (30) days before the current term ends.

## 3. Fees and payment

**3.1 Fees.** Northstar will pay a fixed fee of $38,000 per year, invoiced quarterly, for up to 400,000 translated words. Additional words are billed at the per-word rate in Schedule 1.

**3.2 Payment.** Northstar will pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days of the invoice date.

## 4. Content and data

**4.1 Source content.** Northstar will send Kestrel only product and marketing text written for public release.

**4.2 No customer data.** Kestrel handles no customer personal data under this Agreement, and Northstar will remove any customer details before it sends content.

**4.3 Ownership.** Northstar owns all source content and every translation Kestrel delivers, from the moment it is created.

**4.4 Incident notice.** Kestrel will tell Northstar within seventy-two (72) hours if anyone without permission gains access to unreleased Northstar content.

## 5. Security

**5.1 Handling.** Kestrel will store Northstar content in its encrypted translation workspace, give access only to the linguists assigned to Northstar, and require multi-factor sign-in for that workspace.

**5.2 Machine tools.** Kestrel will not upload Northstar content to public machine translation or AI services without Northstar's written approval.

## 6. Liability and indemnity

**6.1 Cap.** Each party's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months before the claim.

**6.2 IP indemnity.** Kestrel will defend Northstar against any third-party claim that a translation, or a tool Kestrel uses to make it, infringes intellectual property rights, and will pay the damages and settlements finally awarded. This obligation sits outside the cap in Section 6.1.

**6.3 Indirect damages.** Neither party is liable for lost profits or indirect damages.

## 7. Termination

**7.1 For convenience.** Northstar may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice, and will pay for work delivered before the termination date.

**7.2 For cause.** Either party may terminate if the other materially breaches this Agreement and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days of notice.

## 8. General terms

**8.1 Confidentiality.** Each party will protect the other's confidential information during the term and for three (3) years after it ends.

**8.2 Subcontractors.** Kestrel may use freelance linguists who are bound by written confidentiality terms, and remains responsible for their work.

**8.3 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of its business.

**8.4 Governing law.** California law governs this Agreement, and disputes go to the state and federal courts in San Francisco County.
